# Animal Jumping - website

The public page of **Animal Jumping**, a cute 3D party royale: 24 animals, 30 rounds,
7 party modes, bots, online rooms with friends. Android, PC and web.

Everything on the page is the real game: the screenshots and videos were recorded in play,
and the rounds, animals and achievements come from the game's own data
(`assets/data/game.json`).

Static HTML/CSS/JS - no build step. Open `index.html` or serve the folder.
